# Posture — strength is what you are free to do (Mike commissioned 2026-08-17)

**Where this came from.** Mike, thinking aloud at the end of the channel-doctrine
sitting, offered without a commission attached: *"I dont know that its relevant
but you can take any useful insights or ideas from it"*. It was relevant, and he
then ruled it into doctrine.

His four worked cases — home wifi, phone hotspots and BYOD on the wired and
wireless network · secrets easy enough to rotate that exposure is not an issue ·
restores confident enough that data comes back as if nothing happened · disaster
recovery plans actioned without resistance because they are known to work — are
one idea seen four times: **engineer for the failure being cheap, not for the
failure being impossible, and measure the posture by what you are free to do
rather than by what you forbid.**

**Its inverse is the diagnostic, and it is the half that finds things:** a
control that makes you reluctant to act is failing even while nothing has gone
wrong. The cost is invisible because it is paid in things not done — the plan
nobody invokes, the secret nobody rotates, the device nobody connects.

**Mike's rulings, 2026-08-17**, taken with the impacts of each option stated
first:

1. The posture is written as a **section in `PRINCIPLES.md`**, with the docs that
   own the individual cases pointing up at it rather than restating it.
2. It **becomes a test guard work has to pass** — not a posture sitting
   alongside the guard programme. He was told to expect some existing guards to
   fail it and that it would re-rank the open board; he ruled it anyway.
3. The **detection-and-restore capability is audited next** — an honest census of
   what this estate would actually notice and actually restore, separating what
   is proven by exercise from what is assumed.

**The precondition, named because ruling 2 rests on it.** "We will know if
something goes wrong and address it" is doing the load-bearing work in the whole
posture, and it is the part with least mechanism behind it here. Rotation-ease is
provable by rotating; restore-confidence by restoring; *"we would notice"* is the
one that rots silently, and a posture claimed without it is the decorative-guard
shape at estate scale — the confidence reads identical either way. Ruling 3 is
what stops this section being that.
