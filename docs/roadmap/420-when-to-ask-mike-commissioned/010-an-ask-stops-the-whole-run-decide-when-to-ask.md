- [ ] 🎯 **An ask stops the whole run — decide when a session asks, and what
      it does with the rest of the work while the question waits.**
  - [ ] 🔑 **Both costs are real, and the doctrine prices only one.**
    - **Asking too early or too often:** the run halts on the first question,
      the principal may not know it is waiting, and by the time he answers the
      prompt cache has expired — the resume re-pays context, and hours of
      wall-clock are gone. Work that never depended on the answer waited anyway.
    - **Not asking:** wasted work (built on a wrong guess), wrong work (a
      plausible reading that was not his), and unapproved actions (anything on
      the `AUTONOMY.md` floor). His message did not lift the floor; a session
      reading *"don't ask"* as *"decide for me"* would cross it.
  - [ ] 🔎 **Where current doctrine pulls the other way.** The session-open
        prompt's *"don't guess when you could ask"* and `COMMUNICATION.md`'s
        *"not a last resort, and not reserved for the weighty ones"* both make
        the ask the default reflex, with no word on timing — correct when he is
        watching, costly when he is not. `CONCURRENCY.md` already has the piece
        that makes deferral safe: *write the open question into the durable
        record before blocking* — so a parked question outlives the window.
  - [ ] 🤔 **Candidate shape, none chosen:**
    - **Sort each question by what it blocks** — the whole run, one branch of
      it, or nothing (a reversible default exists). Only the first kind
      justifies stopping.
    - **Front-load the run-gating questions** — ask them at the start, while he
      is still there, before the long unattended stretch.
    - **Park and proceed** — record a branch-blocking question durably, carry
      on with everything it does not touch, and put the asks together at the
      end of the run.
    - **Proceed on a stated assumption where the work is recoverable** — so a
      "no" costs a revert, not a re-run; the assumption is named in the record
      and in the end-of-run ask.
    - **Floor items never proceed on assumption** — they queue; the rest of the
      run continues around them.
    - **Presence as a declared mode** — *"I won't be here for several hours"*
      is him declaring he is away. A standing attended/away switch would save
      him re-sending it, and tell a session which timing rule binds.
    - **Make the wait visible** — he said he doesn't always know work is
      waiting. Whether the harness can notify him when a session stops on him
      is unverified at filing, and worth one artefact check before it is relied on.
  - [ ] 📎 **Related, not duplicated:** `280/050` (talk first — a peer session
        may answer what would otherwise wait on him), `300` (recover cheaply —
        provisional work is that posture applied to asking), `410` (repo-less
        sessions have no durable record to park a question in).
