- [ ] 🎯 **The guard layer is consuming the programme — the proportionality
      question, which is Mike's.** Best outcomes is not maximum enforcement,
      and the commission asked for outcomes. Measured 2026-08-15: of **126 open
      board items, roughly 95 are guard, policy, enforcement or review work**,
      against roughly 16 that are new capability. Three-quarters of the open
      work maintains the guard layer.
      **What that layer currently stops: nothing.** The whole-tree floor run
      produces **4,598 findings, and every one sits in a warn-only or advisory
      lane.** The section counts are style-shaped — long sentences and buried
      asides dominate — and no record shows anyone triaging that backlog item by
      item.
      **No guard has ever cleared the bar to become blocking after
      measurement.** The trained-away test has been applied at least three
      times: at a 75% false-positive rate, at a 27% firing rate, and twice
      against a prose lint. All three were refused, correctly on their own
      reasoning. There is no stated threshold, and **no case names a rate that
      passed.** A bar nothing has ever cleared is either a very high bar or a
      test with no positive branch, and the record cannot currently tell which.
      **Guard harm is invisible to the guard.** Every case of a guard being
      unwired or ruled harmful surfaced when a person noticed a downstream
      symptom — unreadable replies, a build stuck red, a claim someone
      re-checked. None flagged itself. The worst ran six days and reprinted
      roughly 123,500 characters of duplicated output before anyone traced it.
      **The question this puts to Mike, and it is not rhetorical.** The four
      builds this section proposes all *add* to the layer. Two of them — the
      before-plane and coverage reporting — plausibly reduce total cost by
      catching things earlier and by making silent non-cover visible. The other
      two add work. Whether the estate should be spending three-quarters of its
      capacity here at all is a proportionality call that has never been put to
      him, and every item in this section is downstream of the answer.
