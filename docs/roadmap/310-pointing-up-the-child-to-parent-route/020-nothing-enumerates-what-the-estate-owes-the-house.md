- [ ] 🎯 **Nothing enumerates what the estate owes the house — the upstream
      mirror of `floorfleet`** `[M][tool]` — the named gap in
      `PROPAGATION.md` § *Pointing up*, and the reason that section says of
      itself that it is rung 1.
      **The asymmetry, stated plainly.** `floorfleet.py` and `pins.py` walk
      every child and answer *is this one current with the house?* No
      instrument answers the mirror — *is anything owed to the house, and has
      it been owed for a while?* An upstream debt is an absence in a repo
      nobody is looking at, which is § *Enumeration, not assumption* aimed at
      the direction it was never pointed at: an absence never raises its hand.
      **What it would have to see**, on the evidence of the instance:
      a pending-upstream line in any child that has outlived the parent item
      it names; a parent board item filed from a child and still open past
      some interval; and a child block whose text has diverged from the
      canonical one (which `stampscan` covers for stamped copies, and misses
      entirely for a rule authored locally — it never claimed to be a copy).
      **Why it is not built here.** It needs a machine-readable marker on the
      child side before it can walk anything, and choosing that marker is a
      convention decision across ten children — the same shape as the floor
      registry, and it wants its own ruling rather than a scanner improvised
      alongside the doctrine that names it. It is also worth pricing against
      `stampscan`'s registry wiring, which is barred on the child-side
      `source=` resolution story (Track D residue) and would answer part of
      this question if it landed.
      **Until it exists** the route rests on a child session filing at the
      moment the rule is earned — a discipline, not a gate.
