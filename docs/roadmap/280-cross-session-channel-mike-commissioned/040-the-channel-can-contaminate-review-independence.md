- [ ] 🚩 **The channel can contaminate review independence, and § The channel
      does not say so** `[S][docs]` — found by using it, 2026-08-17, within the
      hour of the section landing.
      **What happened.** The author of a landed doctrine delta messaged the
      **brief-writer** for that delta's own rule-4 pass, with one factual note
      (the pointer had been rescoped from the commit to paths) and no
      instruction. The brief-writer accepted the fact, changed nothing, and
      **appended a line to the brief's spawn provenance disclosing the contact**
      — that a delta's author had reached the brief-writer post-write, what was
      said, and that nothing changed as a result.
      🔑 **That disclosure was the brief-writer's own initiative, and it is the
      finding.** `REVIEW.md` rule 4's criterion is about who *started or
      instructed* a session; it has nothing to say about a session the author
      merely **talked to** after the fact. The channel makes that contact
      frictionless, so the gap is now reachable in one message where it used to
      require someone to go out of their way.
      **The shape, stated generally:** contact between a delta's author and any
      session in that delta's review chain — brief-writer, reviewer,
      reconciler — is not automatically a breach, and is not automatically fine
      either. A *factual correction* with no instruction is the benign end; a
      framing, a priority, or a "you'll want to look at X" is the other, and
      **neither is distinguishable from outside without a disclosure**.
      **The candidate rule, not written because it is REVIEW.md's to state and
      this finder is the party it would bind:** such contact is permitted and
      **disclosed in the review's provenance by the receiving session** — the
      receiver, not the author, because the author is the interested party and a
      self-report is the weaker artefact. The instance above already did it that
      way, voluntarily, which is the evidence the rule is workable.
      ⚖️ **Why it is queued and not taken here.** The finding is against § The
      channel, which this session authored, and the fix lands in `REVIEW.md`
      rule 4, whose independence criterion this session's own delta is currently
      queued under at `030`. An author widening the rule that governs its own
      pending review is the exact shape rule 4 exists to prevent, whatever the
      merits.
