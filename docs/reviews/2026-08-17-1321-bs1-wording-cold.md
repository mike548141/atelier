## What the work is

One commit, `431f1f7`, four doctrine surfaces plus a CHANGELOG entry. Reviewed
at HEAD.

The board floor check compares the committed index `docs/ROADMAP.md` against
the item files under `docs/roadmap/` and fails a commit whose index is stale.
Before this commit, four surfaces stated that guarantee without qualification
— "makes forgetting it impossible" (`CONCURRENCY.md`), "a commit whose index is
stale … fails" (`tools/README.md`, `tools/board.py` docstring, the board-store
ADR's Decision). The delta qualifies each in place — **on CI unconditionally;
at the hook only when worktree and index agree**, because the hook-plane check
reads the worktree, not the staged plane — and names two consequences: a
rebuilt-but-unstaged index passes the hook, and so does a rebuild that
absorbed a sibling session's dirty item state line. Each surface says the
qualification stands *until the staged-plane check lands* and points at the
funded fix (board item `010/020`).

Surface by surface: `tools/board.py` § *Why the index is committed and
checked* gains one sentence in the middle of an existing paragraph;
`tools/README.md` § **board** gains a clause on the headline sentence and a
forward reference to "the stated residual below"; `docs/method/CONCURRENCY.md`
§ *On a split board* replaces the "impossible" parenthetical with a
five-line one that also adds a **rule** — *a dirty sibling item state line is
a stop for claiming from that checkout, not a stage-yours-alone case*; and the
ADR `docs/decisions/2026-08-15-0610-board-store-per-item-files.md` gains an
appended § *Amended 2026-08-17*, never editing the Decision it qualifies. The
CHANGELOG *Changed* entry restates all four.

## Scope

Widest the work admits. This delta's subject is **a guarantee narrowed to what
the mechanism actually delivers, on every surface that asserted it**, so the
reviewable question is threefold: whether the narrowed statement is *true* of
the mechanism as built (read `tools/floor.py`'s board check and `board.py`'s
check path — which plane reads what, and is "worktree and index agree" the
right name for the condition); whether the four surfaces now say the *same*
thing (four spellings of one qualification drift the way two do); and whether
the one **rule** the delta adds — a dirty sibling state line is a stop for
claiming — is a rule with a home, a mechanism, and a reader, or a sentence
inside a parenthetical inside a paragraph about something else. In scope: the
`tools/README.md` forward reference ("see the stated residual below") — does a
residual section exist below, and does it say what the headline now promises;
whether the ADR's appended-amendment shape is the doc's convention or a first;
whether `until the staged-plane check lands` is a dated, checkable
condition or an open-ended one, and what happens to four surfaces when it does
land; and whether the two consequences named are the whole set (is there a
third way worktree and index disagree that the wording covers or misses —
a partially staged rebuild, a staged item edit with an unstaged index?).

**Non-goals, and neither fences the risk:** the ruling itself — narrow the
wording now, fund the staged-plane build — is the principal's and is not under
review, only its rendering; the staged-plane check (`010/020`) is funded, not
built, and is not under review; and no finding is decided by the reviewer
(rule 3), so the findings go to the principal's next ruling round.
