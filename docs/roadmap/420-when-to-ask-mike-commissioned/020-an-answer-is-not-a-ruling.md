- [ ] 🎯 **An answer to a session's question is not a ruling. Records
      must tell them apart** `[M][doctrine]`. Mike commissioned this,
      2026-10-03. Verbatim:

      > Also add to the board that we need to work on rulings vs me (the
      > principal) answering your questions. My answer to questions are not
      > rulings unless I say otherwise.
      > Rulings are in my words and must be recorded verbatim as I state
      > them with all the relevant context. Questions are something you
      > (claude) have written and often I've picked from the options you gave
      > me in AskUserQuestion rather which is quite different from a ruling
      > and should not carry the same weight

      **The two classes, as he draws them:**
      - **A ruling** is in his own words. It is recorded verbatim, with the
        context it was given in, and it carries the principal's authority.
      - **An answer** is his pick from a question and options a session
        wrote, usually through `AskUserQuestion`. It is the session's framing
        with his selection. It does not carry a ruling's weight unless he
        says it is a ruling.
      **Why it matters now.** Records across the estate use "RULED" for both.
      Two failures are already on the board:
      - `310/080`: a capture credits Mike with a proposal he did not make.
      - `320/510`: a child's record credits him with putting the tool list
        in a private repo. He says he did not, and that it was sessions
        each time.
      A selected option recorded as "Mike ruled X" makes a session's framing
      binding doctrine, which is the self-affirming loop he named the same
      day.
      **Applied at once to this run's own records (2026-10-03).** `200/100`,
      `320/010`, `320/500` and `TOOLBOX.md` now say "answer" where an option
      was picked, and quote him where he spoke.
      **Owed, not built:**
      - (a) a doctrine home and wording. `COMMUNICATION.md` § *Asking for a
        ruling* and the apex's "rulings conditioned" clause are the obvious
        candidates.
      - (b) a record convention that marks which class a decision is (e.g.
        ⚖️ ruling against 💬 answer) and quotes the ruling.
      - (c) a sweep of existing "RULED" records to reclassify the ones that
        were answers. It is large, so do it in parts.
      - (d) whether a guard can check that a "ruled" record carries a
        verbatim quote.
