- [ ] 🔎 **Hand-up: an estate fact learned in a child has no rule sending it
      up to the estate root** `[S][doctrine]`. Handed up by a private child
      on 2026-10-17, under `PROPAGATION.md` § *Pointing up*. **Missing
      rule.** **Evidence, as the child records it:** a session working in
      a private child researched one of the principal's provider accounts
      (its plan, price per line, and entitlements such as hotspotting and
      data sharing) from his mail and the provider's pages. The facts
      landed first in the child's own records. The principal then ruled
      that every such fact belongs in the estate root, with provenance. The
      root homed that rule in its own onramp, but a child session never
      reads the root's onramp. So today the rule binds only sessions that
      happen to start in the root.
      **The gap:** the floor's *Estate resources* line covers one direction
      only: a child **reads** providers, plans, entitlements and inventory
      from the estate root and never re-derives them. It says nothing about
      the other direction: a child session that **learns** such a fact
      (from an invoice, an email, a provider page or the principal's word)
      has no instruction to write it up to the root, with its source and
      date. Without one, estate facts scatter into child records and
      transcripts, which is the re-derivation the read rule exists to stop.
      **Shape the child can name, unevidenced beyond this one instance:**
      the *Estate resources* line gains a write-up clause, for example
      "and a fact about the estate learned in this repo is recorded in the
      estate root, with its provenance, not here". Whether it belongs in
      the floor or in `PROPAGATION.md` is atelier's to decide.
