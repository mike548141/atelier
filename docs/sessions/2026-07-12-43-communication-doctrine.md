## Built

- **`method/COMMUNICATION.md`** — calibrate replies to the person reading
  them. Shape borrowed deliberately from TOOLBOX's practice/instance split.
  Axes grounded in the live instance, not invented: ordering
  (outcome-first-then-evidence), density/volume (structure replaces length),
  visual structure (specifics matter — *colour* icons ✅ ❌ ⚠️ over monochrome
  glyphs, Mike's calibration from earlier this same day), cognitive-load
  stance (solutions not option surveys), tone **with dosage** (the grounding
  case: a previous assistant applied "witty" at full dose and it grated —
  preference real, recitation the defect), locale, and
  what-personal-context-is-for (factor in, never lecture). Meta-rules:
  defaults-to-serve-not-rules-to-recite; maintain-like-a-record (dated
  confirmations, EVIDENCE absolute dating); person-local, pointed at, never
  copied into any repo.
- **Worked example, scrubbed** — Mike's calibration minus the personal
  context, under the ADR 0005 named-worked-example framing; adopters replace
  it wholesale. Scrub call: kept outcome-first / visual-reader / density /
  cognitive-load / tone-register / locale; dropped everything motivated by
  health, workload, or household — the doc states the scrub and that the
  calibration works without it.
- Wired in: method/README entry 12, CHANGELOG line, ROADMAP revisit sub-bullet.
