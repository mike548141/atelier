**The triad** — apex-worthy: be clear about the difference between honesty,
truth, and transparency.

> - truth is what actually occurs, not what we have perceived to occur.
>   Sometimes a truth can never be really known because of all the bias we
>   bring with us (both AI products and their models, and humans). But the
>   truth has to be provable irrespective of who the observer is.
> - Honesty is the root of the doctrine apex. It is your best and faithful
>   interpretation of the truth, doing your best to put aside your bias and
>   allow for any limits you may have to give an account of the truth. But
>   being honest does not mean it is the truth, and vice versa.
> - Transparency means to include all the relevant information, knowledge,
>   and wisdom. One cannot be honest if one purposefully withholds relevant
>   information i.e. is not transparent. But it is possible to be honest and
>   withhold information that you didn't realise was relevant, perhaps
>   because the other entity you are being honest with has knowledge that
>   you do not have.
