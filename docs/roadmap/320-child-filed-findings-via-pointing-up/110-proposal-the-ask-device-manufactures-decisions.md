- [ ] 🎯 **PROPOSAL — § *Asking for a ruling* tells a session HOW to ask and
      never tells it WHETHER there is a decision to ask about, so the device
      manufactures decisions out of non-decisions** `[M][docs]` — filed from a
      private child, 2026-08-25, via § *Pointing up*.

      **Checked the canonical files first**, as § *Check the parent's file
      first* requires, rather than the child's inlined floor block:
      `COMMUNICATION.md` § *Asking for a ruling* owns the ask contract — the
      structured device as default channel, every option with its pros, cons,
      impacts, risks and costs, a recommendation with its reasoning shown, and
      a verified basis. `00-APEX.md` owns *surface a genuine dilemma; never
      silently resolve it*. **Neither says the session must first establish
      that a dilemma exists.** The nearest thing is the apex's word *genuine*,
      which is doing a great deal of unmarked work.

      ## The incident

      A child repository held thirteen third-party suppliers, each carrying one
      of four possible responses to a weakness. The principal had ruled the
      previous day that they be walked **one at a time**. A session did exactly
      that, to the letter of this section: four options per supplier, each with
      trade-offs, a recommendation with reasoning, and a verified basis.

      Seven asks in, the principal stopped it. His words, in order:

      > *"I am wondering if these supplier commercials are the wrong way to ask
      > me. Perhaps you need to give me a paragraph on each to tell me if a
      > weakness or concern was found and then I can say accept as is (never do
      > any work) or if we remediate over time using commercial terms"*

      > *"Perhaps the answer is I look at the report on these 12 first to
      > understand the findings"*

      > *"But what we are doing right now is not working"*

      ## Why this is a doctrine problem and not a session's carelessness

      **The session followed the section and the section is what produced the
      failure.** Three of the thirteen suppliers had been measured and were
      clean — there was nothing to decide about them at all. The section's
      contract has no state for *"there is no decision here, this is a record"*,
      so the session generated four mechanism options anyway, because four
      options with trade-offs is what a well-formed ask looks like under this
      rule. **A rule that specifies the shape of an answer, and not the test
      for whether the question exists, will be satisfied by a well-formed
      question about nothing.**

      Two compounding effects, both visible in the incident:

      - **Evidence gets compressed into the option text.** The section already
        knows the widget is small and says the account must reach the principal
        first. In a harness display mode that shows only the turn's final
        message, that costs a full turn per ask — so a session with twelve asks
        either floods him or violates the ordering. Here the evidence went
        *inside* the options, which is precisely the *"poor language… without
        the context to give it meaning"* his oldest standing instruction names.
      - **The signal that the shape is wrong arrives late and is easy to
        misread as a hard question.** He corrected the framing at ask 1 and the
        session treated it as new input to the same shape rather than as
        evidence against the shape. Nothing in the section tells a session to
        re-examine the *form* when a principal answers the frame instead of the
        question — which is a distinguishable and highly informative event.

      ## What the child did locally, reported rather than worked around silently

      Replaced the twelve asks with one document: a paragraph per supplier
      saying what was found — weakness found · nothing found · not measured —
      proposing nothing, with the decision left to him in his own words. Six
      rulings already taken under the old shape were kept as records.

      **The narrow local fix is not the house rule**, which is why this is filed
      rather than absorbed. The candidate house rule, for atelier to consider
      and word:

      > Before the device, establish that a decision exists. Where the evidence
      > settles the matter, it is a **record**, and a record is reported, never
      > put to a vote. Where several courses are genuinely open, the ask
      > follows this section as written. And where the evidence is **absent**,
      > the honest option set includes *find out first* — which is neither of
      > the two an options-with-trade-offs frame naturally produces.

      ⚠️ **Evidenced, and its scope stated honestly:** one incident, one
      principal, one repository. It is a suspicion with a real incident behind
      it, which § *The duty* makes reportable — not a counted class. Whether it
      generalises is atelier's to decide, and § *When a rule keeps breaking* is
      the place that would know, since it can see whether other children have
      filed the same shape.
